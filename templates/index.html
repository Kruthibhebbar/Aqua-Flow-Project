About
What AquaFlow Is

AquaFlow is an online platform for booking water tankers and managing deliveries. Customers book and follow their bookings from one account, drivers receive and complete assignments, and administrators coordinate the whole process.

Built for Convenient Water Delivery

  • Simple Online Booking

    Choose the water quantity, water type, delivery address and preferred schedule.

  • Booking Status Tracking

    Check the current status of each booking.

  • Delivery Coordination

    See the assigned driver and delivery details once they are available.

  • Booking History

    Review your previous and upcoming bookings.

  • Secure Customer Accounts

    Register with email verification and log in to manage your bookings.

  • Payment Records

    View your payment information and wallet in one place.

How bookings work

You submit a booking, it is reviewed and assigned to a driver, and its status updates in your account until delivery.

Your account, your data

Bookings, payments and profile details are only visible when you are logged in to your own account.

Availability

Delivery depends on the service areas supported at the time and on driver availability for the date you choose.
